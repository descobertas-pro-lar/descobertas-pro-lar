# Descobertas pro Lar — Visual Identity & Styleguide

## Target Audience

**Demographics:**
- Brazilian classes C/D/E (low to lower-middle income)
- Primarily women 25-45, home decision-makers
- Mobile-first (Instagram in-app browser, budget Android phones)
- Looking for practical home products at good prices

**Psychographics:**
- Value-conscious but not cheap — want quality at fair prices
- Practical over aesthetic — products must solve real problems
- Trust personal recommendations over corporate ads
- Responsive to urgency, deals, limited-time offers

**Platform behavior:**
- Scroll quickly — must grab attention in 1-2 seconds
- Share deals with family/friends via WhatsApp
- Click "link na bio" when interested
- Engage with relatable, non-corporate content

| Name | Hex | Usage |

| **Azul Descobertas** (Primary) | `#2E5D8A` | Headers, logo fill, trust elements, body text accents |

| **Creme Fundo** (Background) | `#FDFBF7` | Page/card backgrounds, breathing room |
| **Texto Escuro** (Text) | `#2B2523` | Body text, product names |

### Color Rules
- **Orange is for action/deals only** — never for body text or large backgrounds
- **Blue is the identity** — logo, headers, trust signals
- **Price always in orange** — both crossed-out old price (with strikethrough) and new price
- **Hover/glow effects use orange** — `rgba(255, 122, 61, 0.3)` for shadows
- **White backgrounds for product photos** — lets mix-blend-mode work

## Typography

### Primary Font: **Montserrat**
Available in Canva, Google Fonts, Instagram-safe.

| Style | Weight | Size (mobile) | Usage |
|-------|--------|---------------|-------|
| **Display** | Black (900) | 28-32px | "ACHADO DA SEMANA", campaign titles |
| **Headline** | Bold (700) | 22-26px | Section headers, product names |
| **Body** | Medium (500) | 16-18px | Descriptions, captions |
| **Caption** | Regular (400) | 14px | Fine print, handles |

### Fallback Font: **Inter** or **Poppins**
For when Montserrat isn't available.

### Typography Rules
- **ALL CAPS for prices** — always ("R$69,00" not "r$69,00")
- **ALL CAPS for CTAs** — "LINK NA BIO", "CORRE!"
- **Sentence case for descriptions** — readable, not shouty
- **No thin/light weights** — unreadable on cheap phone screens
- **Line height 1.4-1.5** — breathing room for body text

## Emoji Set (Brand Emojis)

Use these consistently. They are part of the brand identity.

| Emoji | Name | When to Use |
|-------|------|-------------|
| 🔥 | Fire | Titles ("Achado da Semana 🔥"), hot deals, featured products |
| 👆 | Point up | "Link na bio 👆", directing to action |
| 🏠 | House | Bio, niche identifier, home category |
| ✨ | Sparkles | Quality products, "testei e aprovei", special items |
| 💰 | Money bag | Big savings, price drops |

### Emoji Rules
- **Max 2-3 emojis per post/slide** — not a wall of emoji
- **Fire 🔥 is signature** — appears in every "Achado da Semana"
- **Never in product names** — keep names descriptive and clean
- **OK in captions and CTAs** — adds energy
- **Match the website** — fire icon on site = fire emoji in posts

## Design Elements

### Shapes
- **Rounded corners everywhere** — 12-16px radius on cards, buttons
- **Circles for icons/badges** — highlight covers, trust badges
- **No sharp rectangles** — too corporate

### Effects
- **Neon glow on orange elements** — `box-shadow: 0 4px 14px rgba(255, 122, 61, 0.3)`
- **Subtle shadows on cards** — `box-shadow: 0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.08)`
- **No gradients on text** — hard to read
- **Gradient OK on backgrounds** — blue-to-lighter-blue for headers

### Photography Style
- **Product on white/light background** — clean, professional
- **Centered, large** — product is the hero
- **Real product photos from Magalu** — not stock photos
- **No lifestyle shots** — we don't have them, keep it simple

### Layout Patterns
- **Card-based grid** — 3x3 for Top 9, 2x2 for carousels
- **Centered composition** — product in middle, text above/below
- **Clear visual hierarchy** — title → product → price → CTA
- **Whitespace matters** — don't cram everything

## Tone & Voice

### Voice Characteristics
- **Direct** — no fluff, get to the point
- **Enthusiastic** — exclamation points welcome
- **Personal** — "Achei esse" not "Foi encontrado este"
- **Helpful** — sharing a find, not selling
- **Brazilian** — use natural BR Portuguese, not Portugal

### Words to Use
- "Achado", "Achadinho" — not "produto" or "oferta"
- "Corre!" — urgency
- "Olha isso!" — attention
- "Testei e aprovei" — trust
- "Link na bio" — CTA
- "De R$X por R$Y" — price comparison

### Words to Avoid
- Corporate jargon ("soluções", "experiência")
- English when Portuguese works ("sale" → "oferta")
- Passive voice
- "Compre agora" (too pushy) — prefer "Link na bio 👆"

### Caption Structure
```
[Hook — emoji + short punch] 🔥 Achado da semana!

[Product — what it is, key benefit]
Chaleira elétrica 2L, ferve em 3 min

[Price — always highlight the deal]
De R$89 por R$44,90 💰

[CTA — direction]
Link na bio 👆
```
