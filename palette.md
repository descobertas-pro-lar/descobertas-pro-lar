# Descobertas Pro Lar — Color Palette

| Color | Hex | Use |
|---|---:|---|
| Azul Descobertas | `#2E5D8A` | Primary brand color, headers, backgrounds |
| Azul Claro | `#5B9BD5` | Secondary accents and supporting shapes |
| Laranja Oferta | `#FF7A3D` | Prices, CTAs, deal badges, fire icon |
| Laranja Suave | `#FFB347` | Secondary deal highlight |
| Creme Fundo | `#FDFBF7` | Main light background and negative space |
| Texto Escuro | `#2B2523` | Primary text |

## Usage

- Blue is the identity color. Orange is reserved for urgency and offers.
- Use `#FDFBF7` instead of pure white for post backgrounds and open space.
- Keep each social design to this six-color system.
